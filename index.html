Questions & Answers.

Clear answers to common questions about joining, letter submission, FSI, and Review.

Why is joining the WhatsApp group mandatory? +
Joining our WhatsApp group is mandatory because all crucial information and updates are shared there first. We also regularly organize live online meetings inside the group to hear your issues directly and answer your doubts one-on-one. Make sure to fill out the Step 01 Google Form first so your join request is approved.
What do I need to do after downloading my letter? +
After downloading your PDF letter, print two (2) copies, put your self-attestation (signature) and date on both copies, and bring them to the Calcutta University Main Campus on the announced submission date. One copy will be submitted to the CU office, and the second stamped/signed copy will be returned to you as your receipt.
How should I enter my CU Roll Number to find my letter? +
Always enter your official Calcutta University Roll Number with hyphens exactly as printed on your admit card or marksheet (for example: 213411-11-0045), without any extra spaces. If your letter is not listed yet, contact us via the WhatsApp group or email cuunitedstudents@gmail.com.
What is the strict apolitical policy of CU United? +
CU United is a strictly apolitical student collective. We do not involve ourselves in any kind of political activity, party flags, or partisan agendas. If any student is found actively participating or gathering in any political rally, CU United will not represent them.
How does the FSI (Self-Inspection) process work if I suspect an evaluation mistake? +
Follow these exact steps for FSI:
  1. First, apply online for FSI through the official Calcutta University website within the notified window.
  2. When your FSI inspection notice/schedule is published, visit the university to collect and carefully check your answer script.
  3. If you find any unchecked answers, totaling errors, or marking mistakes by the examiner, note down each specific mistake clearly.
  4. Write a formal representation letter addressed to the Controller of Examinations pointing out the exact questions/mistakes where your marks should increase.
  5. Important Preference: First preference is always given when the representation letter is written or endorsed by a Professor. If a professor does not provide the letter, you can write and submit the letter yourself.
How does the Review (Re-evaluation) process work? +
In the Review process, a professor assigned by Calcutta University re-evaluates your answer script from scratch. Please keep in mind that depending on the re-evaluation of your answers, your marks can either increase or decrease.
