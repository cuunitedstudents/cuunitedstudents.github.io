Our Motive & Principles

Many students across Calcutta University face genuine administrative, registration, and exam-schedule hurdles that threaten their academic progress. CU United brings affected students together onto one transparent platform to verify facts, collect student data responsibly, and submit formal representations to university authorities.

Strictly Apolitical No political flags, external agendas, or partisan banners—only genuine student welfare.
Verified Information Only authentic circulars, confirmed timelines, and documented updates are shared here.
Constructive & Formal Focused on respectful, well-documented representations to the Hon'ble Vice Chancellor and university offices.
