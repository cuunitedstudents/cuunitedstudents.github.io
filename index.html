Our Motive & Core Objectives

Apolitical & Constructive

CU United unites Calcutta University students on a transparent, non-partisan platform to address genuine academic and evaluation hurdles through respectful, well-documented representations to university authorities.

01

One-Year CBCS Extension

Securing a 1-year extension for CBCS batch students through constructive administrative dialogue so every student gets a fair chance to complete their degree.

02

Dedicated FSI & Review Cell

Advocating for a dedicated, accountable FSI (Self-Inspection) and Review cell so answer-script scrutiny becomes faster and far more transparent for students.

03

CCF Batch Student Support

Building a continuous, apolitical student support network to resolve academic and administrative challenges faced by current and future CCF batch students.

Major Achievement

Special Review Granted for Backlog Students

Following our continuous constructive dialogue with university authorities, the Hon'ble Vice Chancellor approved the Special Review opportunity for backlog students.

Direct Student Impact

FSI Guidance & Two Students Passed Without Re-Sitting Exams

Through our step-by-step guidance on the FSI (Self-Inspection) and review process, two students successfully cleared their backlog papers without having to sit for another examination—saving a full academic year for each of them.

3 Joint Administrative Meetings on the Extension Issue

Over the past year at Calcutta University, we held 3 formal meetings with the Hon'ble Vice Chancellor, the Controller of Examinations, and the Registrar—with the Pro-Vice Chancellor (Academic) also joining the third meeting—to press for the CBCS extension.

Mass Submission of Student Representation Letters

Collected and mass-submitted documented representation letters signed by affected students to university offices so individual grievances are placed officially on record.
